Chatbot Prompts
Use prompts like these in the chat widget:

Product discovery:

“Show me running shoes”
“What electronics do you have?”
“Find me backpacks under $100”
“Recommend something for home office”
“Do you have waterproof jackets?”
“Show me clothes for gym”

Semantic/product-detail search:

“I need something lightweight for travel”
“Show products with good durability”
“Find breathable workout tops”
“Do you have items with cotton or spandex?”
“Show me products with DDR5”
“Find NVMe storage options”

Top-rated / best-reviewed:

“What are your top rated shoes?”
“Show me the best reviewed clothing”
“Which is the most popular bag?”
“Recommend the best electronics”

Compare products:

“Compare running shoes and trail running shoes”
“What’s the difference between backpack A and backpack B?”
“Which is better, this keyboard or that speaker?”
“Help me choose between two jackets”

Add to cart:

“Add the yoga mat to cart”
“Add 2 running shoes”
“Can you add a backpack for me?”
“Add all of them”
“Add this to my cart”

Add with options:

“Add the hoodie in size M”
“Add trail running shoes in black UK 9”
“I want the t-shirt, size L, color white”

Update quantity:

“Change the quantity of the hoodie to 3”
“Reduce the shoes to 1”
“Add one more of the backpack”
“Increase the keyboard quantity to 2”

Remove / clear cart:

“Remove the hoodie from my cart”
“Delete the running shoes”
“Take out the backpack”
“Clear my cart”
“Remove everything from cart”

Cart questions

“What’s in my cart?”
“Show my cart”
“How much is in my cart?”
“What is my cart total?”

Bundles / setups

“Build a bundle with clothes”
“Create a workout bundle”
“Suggest a home office setup”
“Put together a gaming PC bundle”
“What should I get for a travel bundle?”

Order history / reorder

“Show my order history”
“What did I order before?”
“Repeat my last order”
“Reorder the same things again”
“Buy the same as last time”

Search In Page
The page search supports two modes from page.tsx:

Keyword search

“headphones”
“backpack”
“shirt”
“DDR5 RAM”
“water bottle”

AI Search / semantic search

“lightweight travel bag”
“comfortable running shoes”
“durable workout clothing”
“noise cancelling for office”
“storage for gaming PC”
“breathable sportswear”

You can also combine page search with category filters:

Category: Clothing, Search: “hoodie”
Category: Electronics, Search: “wireless”
Category: Sports, Search: “trail running”
